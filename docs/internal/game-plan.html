The proposal

Not "an AI that picks stocks." IV Desk is a disciplined premium-selling engine with an LLM judgment layer on top and a live dashboard anyone can watch operate. It trades defined-risk structures — iron condors and vertical credit spreads, 1–3 DTE — on SPY, QQQ and IWM, and it only sells premium when the options surface says premium is genuinely rich and dealer hedging supports the structure.

The competition draws ~2,000 entrants. Almost all will submit some version of the same thing: a bull/bear multi-agent that sells SPY credit spreads on an RSI signal. We win by being different in the three places that matter.

Why this wins — three layers

01 — THE MOAT

Options-native signal

VRP + GEX, not RSI. We measure implied vs forecast realized vol, and aggregate dealer gamma from open interest. ~1,900 of 2,000 wrap a price indicator in options. Almost none know what dealer gamma is.

02 — THE FILTER

It actually runs 6 days

Deterministic exit manager, circuit breakers, event blackouts. The "fresh account + verifiable P&L" rule washes out ~80% of entrants. Reliable execution alone is top-20%.

03 — THE STAGE

You can watch it think

Live "trading floor" dashboard, a prediction ledger grading every thesis, debate transcripts. Clones ship a repo and a screen recording. This wins Presentation and feeds Social.

Decisions

Locked Settled by the Day-0 probes

  • Iron condor as a single 4-leg mleg order — confirmed on paper
  • feed=indicative for greeks + IV (~2s fresh; OPRA is a paid add-on)
  • Prefer 1–3 DTE; 0DTE only with wider stop buffers
  • Transport: httpx + REST; CLI wired as the secondary path
  • Universe: SPY, QQQ, IWM — all have daily expirations through the window
  • Risk posture: consistent core + small directional satellite

Open Needs a call

  • Regime-adaptive desk vs strict discipline. Current low-vol tape (ATM IV 6–10%) makes the pure signal sit on its hands. Recommendation: adaptive — sell condors when VRP is rich, buy cheap directional debit spreads when gamma is negative and the tape trends. Keeps discipline, guarantees a track record.
  • Final parameter calibration Monday with real open-market quotes: vrp_min, short_delta, min_credit_frac.

Risks we're not hiding from

A low-volatility week

Thin premium means P&L may be small even when the strategy is "right." Mitigated by the adaptive mode and the directional satellite, not eliminated.

Six days is a variance lottery

On the P&L axis specifically, one trend day can erase a week of condor gains. This is why we don't stake the project on that axis.

Scope in three days, two people

LLM desk + dashboard + content is tight. The cut-list is explicit: reflection loop first, then the ensemble collapses to one model, then the debate collapses to the Desk Head reading the signal directly. The core loop and risk gates never get cut.

The debate structure isn't original by itself

The ai-hedge-fund repo already does bull/bear/judge and many will copy it. Our originality budget is spent on the signal — the vol-surface grounding — not the theater.
